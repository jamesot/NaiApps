01 · THE RING

Have I hit today's target?

A big ring fills as you earn and shows exactly what's left: "KSh 750 to go". M-Pesa till payments land in the ring by themselves. Cash and fuel take two taps. Your target sets itself: your installment, plus your average fuel, plus a 15% buffer.

Your loan drawn as a road: installments paid, installments left, and the date the bike becomes yours. Pay an installment by M-Pesa from the app, keep your streak going, and see how a little extra each day brings ownership closer.

Built for the road.

Everything a rider needs from the stage, nothing they don't.

Automatic daily target

Installment + 7-day average fuel + 15% buffer, recalculated every night. Edit it any time; one tap puts it back on auto.

M-Pesa in, M-Pesa out

Till payments fill the ring live. Paste an M-Pesa SMS as a fallback. Pay installments and save with STK push. Get paid by QR.

Works offline

Entries save on the phone first and sync when there's network. Today and Progress run fully from the device.

Widget & glance ring

A home-screen widget and an optional notification-bar ring, so progress is one look away without opening the app.

Alerts that matter

When M-Pesa lands, when an installment is confirmed, and 14/7/1 days before insurance, licence, NTSA inspection or county sticker expire.

Insights & streaks

Gold-hours heatmap, best days, fuel KSh/day and km/l, honest net-vs-gross, and installment streak badges at 30, 90 and 180.

Stage crew mode

Join your stage with a code. Leaderboards count days you hit your target, never money.

One-press SOS

Sends your location by SMS or WhatsApp to two people you choose. Read on the phone at press time, never sent to a server.

English & Kiswahili

Full Kiswahili with a little Sheng flavour, plus fingerprint or face unlock and a PIN.

Under the hood

A monorepo: a Flutter rider app and a Next.js PWA that share one backend and one account.

Mobile
Flutter · Riverpod · go_router · SQLite offline ledger · WidgetKit & Android widget
Web
Next.js 14 PWA + desktop Partner dashboard · Tailwind · TanStack Query
Data
PostgreSQL 16 · Drizzle ORM · native Row Level Security, proven by tests
Payments
Safaricom Daraja: C2B auto-capture, STK push, Dynamic QR
Realtime & push
Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY → SSE · FCM HTTP v1
Security
Phone + PIN (bcrypt), short-lived JWT with rotating refresh, lockout, biometric unlock
Money
Integer cents, fuel in millilitres, Africa/Nairobi time everywhere
Deploy
Docker Compose: app + Postgres + Caddy + cron backups
