Delete your BodaPro account

For the BodaPro web app and BodaPro for Android and iOS, by Crystal Hub Solutions Limited.

In the app or on the web

  1. Open BodaPro and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your PIN.

If you can't sign in

Email tha_ros@icloud.com from any address, with the phone number on your account. We will confirm it is your number before deleting anything, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Straight away and for good: your account, phone number, PIN, name, earnings, expenses, fuel, bike loan and payments, savings, renewal dates, daily target, no-work days, emergency contacts, M-Pesa till number, push tokens, crew membership and any financier access you gave. The copy on your phone is wiped too.

What is kept

Nothing in our database. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within 30 days. Safaricom keeps its own records of M-Pesa payments under its own policy.

Accounts kept only on your phone

If you never connected BodaPro to our servers, we hold nothing about you. Delete the account in the app (Settings → Delete account), or uninstall the app.
