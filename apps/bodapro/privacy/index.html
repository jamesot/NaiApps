Privacy policy

BodaPro web app and BodaPro for Android and iOS. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Who we are

BodaPro is run by Crystal Hub Solutions Limited. We decide how your information is used, so we are its data controller under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Write to us at tha_ros@icloud.com about anything in this policy.

What we store

Your phone number, a hashed PIN, your name if you give it, and the money records you enter or that arrive by M-Pesa: earnings, expenses, fuel litres, your bike loan and its payments, savings, renewal dates, the daily target you set and your two emergency contacts. We also store any days you mark as no work, to keep your daily streak going. Amounts are stored to the cent so your totals are exact. A copy of your earnings, expenses, no-work days and loan progress, and any saved QR codes, stay on your device so the app works offline. Logging out or deleting your account removes them from that device, with one exception: if you log out with no connection, or with records that are not backed up, they stay on the device, locked behind your PIN, until you log in again.

You can use the BodaPro phone app without connecting to our servers. Your account and records then stay only on your phone, and we hold nothing about you until you choose to connect. If you lose that phone, we cannot recover them. To check your PIN with no connection, your device keeps a scrambled version of it, never the PIN itself.

Why we use it

Only to run the features you use: your totals, target, streak, loan progress, payments and reminders. Our legal basis is providing the service you signed up for, and your consent for the things you switch on yourself: financier access, crews and notifications. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we build no profile of you for anyone else.

Getting paid by QR

If you add your M-Pesa till number, we store it and show customers a QR code that pays your till directly. BodaPro never holds your money. When Safaricom confirms a payment we record the amount and receipt as an earning. We do not keep the customer's name or phone number.

Location — never stored

BodaPro has no location field anywhere in its database. When you press SOS, your phone reads its location once and puts it in an SMS or WhatsApp message to your two emergency contacts. That location never reaches BodaPro. The app does not track you in the background.

Financiers and SACCOs

A financier sees nothing about you unless you grant them access in Settings, and you can revoke it at any time — revocation is immediate. Even with access they see only summary signals: days active, a consistency score and your installment streak. Never individual trips, amounts, transactions or location. Your daily streak and no-work days are never shown to them.

Crew leaderboards

If you join a crew, its members see how many days you hit your target. Never how much you earned, and never your daily streak.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can tell you when an M-Pesa payment lands, when an installment is confirmed, and when a renewal is due. Notification text includes amounts; on Android it is hidden on a locked screen. Logging out removes the token. If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it itself: nothing goes through us or Google, and it contains no amounts.

Service providers

Safaricom (M-Pesa payments you start, and confirmations of payments to your till), Africa's Talking (renewal SMS), Google Firebase (push notifications) and the company that hosts our servers. Each one gets only what it needs to do that job.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Deleting your account removes it and every record linked to it from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within 30 days. Safaricom keeps its own records of M-Pesa payments under its own policy.

How we protect it

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your PIN is stored only as a one-way hash. In our database each rider's records are walled off, so one account cannot read another's. On your phone, records stay in the app's private storage and your sign-in keys in the phone's secure storage.

Your rights

You can download your money records as CSV from Settings, ask us for a copy of anything else we hold about you, ask us to correct anything that is wrong, delete your account, and withdraw any consent you gave, such as financier access or notifications, at any time. You can also object to how we use your data. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Deleting your account

In the app or on the web: Settings → Delete account, then confirm with your PIN. If you can no longer sign in, see how to delete your account.

Age

BodaPro is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it.

Changes

The date at the top shows when this policy last changed. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
